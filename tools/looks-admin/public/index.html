Fairy Peony

Shop the Look Publisher

Paste the Pinterest image link and the product links from fairypeony.com, choose the category, and the app will fetch each product's name, photo and price, save the look, and rebuild the Shop the Look page. When you are happy, press Push live to send it to GitHub.
